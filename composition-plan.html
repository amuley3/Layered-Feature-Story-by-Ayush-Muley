Element Content purpose Normal-flow baseline Chosen technique and why Containing block / stacking context Fallback or disengagement Required test
Issue badge Shows the automotive edition label. Would normally appear under the introduction. I picked absolute positioning because it keeps it in the top corner. Feature header, which has position relative. Changes back to normal flow on small screens. I tested on desktop and mobile with a long title.
Story guide Helps readers find different story sections. Appears before the article in source order. I picked sticky positioning because it keeps it visible while scrolling. Main grid area and its containing block. Changes to static positioning on smaller screens. I scrolled through the article and checked the section boundaries.
Pull quote Highlights an important idea in the story. Appears between regular paragraphs. I picked relative positioning and a higher layer because it allows a controlled overlap if spacing is adjusted. Its normal article position. Normal margins on smaller screens. I checked that it does not cover paragraphs or links.
Feature image Introduces the subject of the article. Appears as a regular figure with a caption. I picked rounded corners to improve the appearance. And also clip-path adds a slightly angled edge. Feature figure. Without clip-path, the image remains rectangular with rounded corners. I disabled clip-path and checked that the image is still visible.
Shape portrait Adds a visual break in the future of cars section. Appears as a regular image before the paragraph. I picked float and shape-outside to make the text wrap around the circular image. The future section, which uses flow-root. Without shape-outside, text wraps around the regular float. On narrow screens it becomes a normal image. I disabled shape-outside and checked text wrapping.
Story documentation Provides assessment information and project links. Appears after the article. I picked grid column spanning so it places across the main layout. Main grid container. Uses one column on smaller screens. I checked the layout at 320px, 768px, and 1280px.
